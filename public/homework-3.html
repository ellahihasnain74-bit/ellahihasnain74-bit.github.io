About Brawlhalla

Brawlhalla is a 2D platform fighter game in which you can fight your opponents 1v1, 2v2, or 3v3 as they added recently as permanent gamemode. You can get all the info at Brawlhalla Wiki.

The Different Gamemodes

Signatures/Special attacks

You can get all the information at Brawlhalla.wiki/Attacks

Each character has 6 unique special moves, 3 per weapon. Called sigantures, you have three for unarmed aswell, but every character has unarmed. There are three different types of special moves, Neutral signature, side siganture, and down signature for each weapon, including unarmed. Each signature varies in damage, force, and stun time per character and signature.

Stances

You can get all the information at Brawlhalla,wiki/Stances.

There are four different stance types, where you can add one or two points to one stance to make your character better.

  • Speed
    • Speed determines how fast your character can move/run. This will also effect your speed in the air.
  • Strength
    • Strength, quite obvious from the name determines how much damage your attacks do, light attack or signatures.
  • Defence
    • Defence, quite opposite from strength, determines how much damage, or, how fast your character dies.
  • Dexterity
    • Dexterity determines how fast you can attack after an attack, it also controls how fast you loose your weapon/getting disarmed.
